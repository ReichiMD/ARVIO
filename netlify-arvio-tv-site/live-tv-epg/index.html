ARVIO supplies no channels

You must connect a lawful service or playlist you are authorized to use. EPG coverage, channel uptime, archive depth and catch-up playback are controlled by that provider. ARVIO cannot create missing guide data or archive streams.

Common questions

Live TV and EPG FAQ

Does ARVIO include live TV channels?

No. ARVIO does not provide channels, playlists or streams. You must connect an authorized provider or playlist.

Can ARVIO preserve the provider's channel order?

Yes. ARVIO can retain provider group and channel order by default, while still allowing profile-specific visibility and ordering controls.

Why is catch-up unavailable for a program?

Catch-up requires the provider to expose archive capability and a valid playback URL for that channel and program. EPG history alone does not guarantee playable catch-up.
